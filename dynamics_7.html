Rotational Dynamics & Torque Quiz (Calculus-Based)

This assessment evaluates core concepts in rotational dynamics, including \(\tau = I\alpha\), integration for moments of inertia, time-dependent torques, and angular momentum changes.

1. A thin rod of length \( L \) and total mass \( M \) has a non-uniform linear mass density given by \( \lambda(x) = \gamma x^2 \), where \( x \) is the distance from the left end. What is the moment of inertia \( I \) of the rod when rotated about an axis passing through its left end \( (x=0) \)?

2. A flywheel with a fixed moment of inertia \( I \) is subjected to a time-varying net torque \( \tau(t) = \tau_0 e^{-bt} \). If the flywheel starts from rest at \( t = 0 \), what is its angular velocity \( \omega(t) \) as a function of time?

3. The angular position of a rotating rigid body of moment of inertia \( I \) is described by \( \theta(t) = a t^3 - b t^2 \). What is the net torque \( \tau(t) \) acting on the body as a function of time?

4. A particle of mass \( m \) moves in the xy-plane with a position vector \( \vec{r}(t) = (ct^2)\hat{i} + (dt)\hat{j} \). If a net force \( \vec{F} \) acts on the particle, what is the net torque \( \vec{\tau} \) acting on the particle about the origin at any time \( t \)?

5. A uniform solid cylinder of mass \( M \) and radius \( R \) \( (I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2) \) is free to rotate about its central axis. A cord wrapped around it is pulled with a time-dependent force \( F(t) = kt^2 \). Find the angular acceleration \( \alpha(t) \) of the cylinder.

6. A rigid wheel with moment of inertia \( I \) experiences an angular velocity-dependent resisting torque due to fluid friction given by \( \tau_{drag} = -k\omega \). If the wheel has an initial angular velocity \( \omega_0 \), how much time does it take for its angular velocity to drop to \( \frac{1}{2}\omega_0 \)?

7. A non-uniform flat disk of radius \( R \) has a surface mass density that depends on radial distance: \( \sigma(r) = \alpha r \). What is the total moment of inertia \( I \) of this disk for rotation about a perpendicular axis through its center?

8. A constant net torque \( \tau_0 \) acts on a system whose moment of inertia is changing with time such that \( I(t) = I_0 + \beta t \). Assuming the system starts from rest, find the expression for its angular momentum \( L(t) \) at time \( t \).

9. A thin plate extends along the x-axis from \( x=0 \) to \( x=L \). It experiences a force per unit length acting perpendicular to the plate given by \( f(x) = F_0 \cos\left(\frac{\pi x}{2L}\right) \). What is the total torque \( \tau \) exerted by this force about the pivot at \( x=0 \)?

10. An object with a moment of inertia \( I \) accelerates from an initial angular velocity \( \omega = 0 \) to a final angular velocity \( \omega = \Omega \) under a torque that depends on the angular position, \( \tau(\theta) = \kappa \theta \). Through what total angle \( \theta_f \) must the object rotate to reach this speed?
