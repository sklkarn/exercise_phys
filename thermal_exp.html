Thermal Expansion Quiz (Calculus-Based)

1. The exact thermodynamic definition of the coefficient of linear thermal expansion $\alpha$ for a material of length $L$ under constant pressure is expressed as which partial derivative?

2. A rod has a temperature-dependent linear expansion coefficient given by $\alpha(T) = a + bT$, where $a$ and $b$ are constants. If the rod has an initial length $L_0$ at $T = 0$, what is its exact length $L(T)$ after being heated to temperature $T$?

3. For an isotropic solid body, the volume thermal expansion coefficient $\beta = \frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P$ is related to the linear coefficient $\alpha$ via calculus differentials. Assuming small strains, how are $\beta$ and $\alpha$ related?

4. An anisotropic crystal has different linear coefficients of thermal expansion along its principal orthogonal axes: $\alpha_x$, $\alpha_y$, and $\alpha_z$. What is the exact total differential expression for the change in volume $dV$ as a function of temperature change $dT$?

5. A solid bar of length $L$, Young's modulus $Y$, and linear expansion coefficient $\alpha$ is rigidly clamped between two immovable walls. If the temperature is raised by $dT$, what is the differential thermal stress $d\sigma$ induced within the bar?

6. For an ideal gas at constant pressure $P$, the volume satisfies $V = \frac{nRT}{P}$. By evaluating the partial derivative $\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P$, what is the functional volume expansion coefficient $\beta$ of an ideal gas?

7. A non-uniform rod of length $L_0$ expands such that its linear expansion coefficient varies along its position coordinate $x$ as $\alpha(x) = \alpha_0 \frac{x}{L_0}$. If the entire rod experiences a uniform temperature increase $\Delta T$, what is the total integrated change in length $\Delta L$?

8. If the density $\rho$ of a substance is given by $\rho = \frac{M}{V}$, which differential calculus expression relates the fractional change in density to the volume expansion coefficient $\beta$ during heating?

9. A circular hoop of initial radius $R_0$ has a linear thermal expansion coefficient $\alpha$. If its temperature changes by a differential amount $dT$, the change in its circumference $C$ satisfies $dC = C \alpha \, dT$. What is the resulting differential change in its inner area $dA$?

10. The relation $\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P = -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial P}\right)_T \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_V$ stems from the triple product rule of calculus. How can the volume expansion coefficient $\beta$ be expressed using isothermal compressibility $\kappa = -\frac{1}{V}\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial P}\right)_T$?
