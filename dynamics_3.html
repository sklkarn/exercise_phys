Calculus: Force & Equilibrium Quiz

1. A particle moves along the x-axis under the influence of a conservative force field. Its potential energy function is given by U(x) = x³ - 3x² - 9x + 5. At what value of x > 0 is the particle in a state of stable equilibrium?

Explanation: Equilibrium occurs where F = -dU/dx = 0.
dU/dx = 3x² - 6x - 9 = 0 → x² - 2x - 3 = 0 → (x - 3)(x + 1) = 0. Thus, x = 3 or x = -1.
For stable equilibrium, the potential energy must be at a local minimum, meaning d²U/dx² > 0.
d²U/dx² = 6x - 6. At x = 3, d²U/dx² = 18 - 6 = 12 > 0. Since the question asks for x > 0, x = 3 is the correct answer.

2. A variable force F(x) = 4x³ - 2x acts on an object. What is the total work done by this force to move the object from x = 1 meter to x = 2 meters?

Explanation: Work done is the integral of force with respect to position: W = ∫ F(x) dx from x=1 to x=2.
W = ∫ (4x³ - 2x) dx = [x⁴ - x²] from 1 to 2.
W = (2⁴ - 2²) - (1⁴ - 1²) = (16 - 4) - (1 - 1) = 12 - 0 = 12 Joules.

3. The force required to stretch a non-linear spring is given by F(x) = kx + bx², where k = 10 N/m and b = 3 N/m². If the spring is in equilibrium under an external load after being stretched 2 meters from its natural length, what is the magnitude of the external force?

Explanation: In static equilibrium, the sum of forces equals zero, so the external force must exactly balance the restoring force of the spring at x = 2 m.
Evaluate F(2) = 10(2) + 3(2)² = 20 + 3(4) = 20 + 12 = 32 N.

4. A heavy rope of length L and total mass M hangs vertically from a ceiling. Using calculus, determine the tension T(y) in the rope as a function of the distance y measured upward from the bottom free end of the rope.

Explanation: Consider a small segment of rope dy at a distance y from the bottom. The linear mass density is λ = M/L.
The tension T(y) at a distance y must support the weight of the entire section of rope below it (from 0 to y).
Mass of the segment below y is m(y) = ∫ λ dy [from 0 to y] = λy = (M/L)y.
Therefore, the balancing tension force is T(y) = m(y)g = (M/L) * g * y.

5. The potential energy of a two-atom system is modeled by the Lennard-Jones style relation U(r) = a/r² - b/r, where a and b are positive constants and r is the distance between atoms. Find the equilibrium separation distance r₀ where the net force between the atoms is zero.

Explanation: Force is related to potential energy by F = -dU/dr. Net force is zero at equilibrium.
U(r) = a * r⁻² - b * r⁻¹
dU/dr = -2a * r⁻³ + b * r⁻² = -2a/r³ + b/r²
Set dU/dr = 0 → 2a/r³ = b/r²
Multiply both sides by r³ → 2a = b * r → r₀ = 2a / b.
