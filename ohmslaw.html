2. Current density J is related to the drift velocity v_d by:

J = n e / v_d

I = J ⋅ A (or I = J A for uniform J)
I = J / A
I = n e v_d
I = n e v_d A

J = σ E

ρ = 1/σ

V = I R
I = V / R
