Physics Quiz

Topic: Kinematics, Projectile Motion, and Circular Motion

Section 1: 1D Kinematics

1. A car accelerates from rest at a constant rate of 3.0 m/s² for 4.0 seconds. How far does it travel?

2. Which of the following pairs consists of one scalar quantity and one vector quantity?

3. An object is thrown vertically upward, reaches its highest point, and falls back down. What is its acceleration at the highest point?

Section 2: Projectile Motion

4. A ball is kicked horizontally off a cliff with a velocity of 15 m/s. If the cliff is 20 meters high, approximately how long does it take for the ball to hit the ground? (Use g = 9.8 m/s²)

5. At the peak of a projectile’s trajectory, which component of its velocity is equal to zero?

6. Neglecting air resistance, the horizontal acceleration of a projectile is always:

7. To achieve the maximum horizontal range over level ground, a projectile should ideally be launched at what angle?

Section 3: Circular Motion

8. An object moves in a circle of radius 5.0 m at a constant speed of 10 m/s. What is the magnitude of its centripetal acceleration?

9. In uniform circular motion, the direction of the net centripetal acceleration is always:

10. If an object moving in a circle doubles its speed while keeping the radius constant, its centripetal acceleration changes by what factor?

Answer Key & Explanations

  • 1. B - Using d = 0.5 * a * t² -> 0.5 * 3 * 4² = 24 m.
  • 2. C - Speed is scalar (magnitude only); Acceleration is a vector (magnitude and direction).
  • 3. B - Gravity never turns off; acceleration remains a constant 9.8 m/s² downward.
  • 4. B - Vertical motion: t = √(2h/g) -> √(40/9.8) ≈ 2.02 s.
  • 5. B - At the peak, it stops rising but keeps moving horizontally.
  • 6. A - Gravity only pulls downward, leaving horizontal acceleration at zero.
  • 7. B - 45 degrees maximizes the balance between air time and forward velocity.
  • 8. C - Using a_c = v² / r -> 10² / 5 = 20 m/s².
  • 9. C - Centripetal means "center-seeking".
  • 10. D - Since a_c ∝ v², doubling the speed squares the acceleration factor (2² = 4).
