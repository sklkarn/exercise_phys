Rigid Body Dynamics Quiz (Calculus-Based)

1. A thin rod of length $L$ and total mass $M$ has a non-uniform linear mass density given by $\lambda(x) = \gamma x$, where $x$ is the distance from one end ($x=0$) and $\gamma$ is a constant. What is the moment of inertia of the rod about an axis perpendicular to the rod passing through $x=0$?

2. The angular momentum $\vec{L}$ of a system of particles about a given origin is changing over time. If $\vec{r}_i$ is the position vector and $\vec{p}_i$ is the linear momentum of the $i$-th particle, which of the following expressions correctly defines the fundamental calculus relationship between net external torque $\vec{\tau}_{\text{net}}$ and angular momentum?

3. A rigid body with a time-dependent moment of inertia $I(t)$ is rotating about a fixed axis under zero net external torque. If its angular position is given by $\theta(t)$, which differential equation accurately describes the conservation of its angular momentum?

4. A solid cylinder of radius $R$ and mass $M$ has a density that varies with the radial distance $r$ from its central longitudinal axis as $\rho(r) = \rho_0 \left(1 - \frac{r}{R}\right)$. To calculate its total moment of inertia about this axis, which integral setup is correct?

5. A constant power $P$ is applied to a flywheel having a constant moment of inertia $I$. Starting from rest ($\omega = 0$ at $t = 0$), what is the angular velocity $\omega(t)$ as a function of time?

6. A particle of mass $m$ moves along a straight line path defined by the vector equation $\vec{r}(t) = (vt)\hat{i} + b\hat{j}$, where $v$ and $b$ are constants. What is the time derivative of the particle's angular momentum ($\frac{d\vec{L}}{dt}$) evaluated about the origin?

8. A uniform thin disk of radius $R$ and mass $M$ lies in the $xy$-plane. Using calculus integration, the moment of inertia about its central axis ($z$-axis) is found to be $I_z = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$. According to the Perpendicular Axis Theorem, what is the integrated moment of inertia about the $x$-axis ($I_x$)?

9. A time-dependent torque $\tau(t) = \alpha t^2$ (where $\alpha$ is a constant) is applied to a stationary rigid body with moment of inertia $I$. What is the expression for the angular momentum $L(t)$ at any time $t > 0$?
