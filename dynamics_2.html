Physics Quiz: Static & Translational Equilibrium

1. A block of mass \( m \) is suspended by two light strings. String A is horizontal and attached to a wall, while String B is at an angle of \( 60^\circ \) to the horizontal ceiling. If the block is in static equilibrium, what is the tension in the horizontal string (String A)?

  • A) \( mg \sin(60^\circ) \)
  • B) \( mg \cos(60^\circ) \)
  • C) \( mg \cot(60^\circ) \)
  • D) \( mg \tan(60^\circ) \)

2. For an object to be in complete static equilibrium, which of the following conditions must be satisfied?

  • A) Only \( \sum \vec{F} = 0 \)
  • B) Only \( \sum \vec{\tau} = 0 \)
  • C) Both \( \sum \vec{F} = 0 \) and \( \sum \vec{\tau} = 0 \)
  • D) The velocity must be constant and non-zero.

3. A uniform beam of length \( L \) and weight \( W \) is supported by a pivot at one end and a vertical rope at the other end. If a person weighing \( 2W \) stands at the exact center of the beam, what is the tension in the rope?

  • A) \( 0.5W \)
  • B) \( 1.0W \)
  • C) \( 1.5W \)
  • D) \( 3.0W \)

4. Three forces act on a particle in translational equilibrium: \( \vec{F_1} = (3\hat{i} + 4\hat{j}) \, \text{N} \), \( \vec{F_2} = (-2\hat{i} + 5\hat{j}) \, \text{N} \), and \( \vec{F_3} \). What is the magnitude of the third force \( \vec{F_3} \)?

  • A) \( \sqrt{82} \, \text{N} \)
  • B) \( \sqrt{10} \, \text{N} \)
  • C) \( 9 \, \text{N} \)
  • D) \( 1 \, \text{N} \)

5. A sphere of weight \( W \) rests in a smooth V-shaped trough where the sides are inclined at \( 30^\circ \) and \( 60^\circ \) to the horizontal. Which side exerts a greater normal force on the sphere?

  • A) The \( 30^\circ \) side
  • B) The \( 60^\circ \) side
  • C) Both exert equal force \( (W/2) \)
  • D) It depends on the radius of the sphere.
