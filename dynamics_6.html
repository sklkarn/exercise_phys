Rotational Equilibrium Quiz (Calculus-Based)

Test your understanding of rotational equilibrium, torque integration, and center of mass distributions. Choose the best answer for each of the 10 problems.

1. A non-uniform rod of length \( L \) lies along the x-axis from \( x = 0 \) to \( x = L \). Its linear mass density is given by \( \lambda(x) = \alpha x \), where \( \alpha \) is a constant. At what distance from \( x = 0 \) should a single pivot be placed so that the rod balances horizontally in static equilibrium?

2. A uniform beam of length \( L \) and mass \( M \) is held horizontally. A distributed vertical load acts on the beam such that the downward force per unit length is \( q(x) = kx \), where \( x \) is measured from the left support. What is the total clockwise torque exerted by this distributed load about the left end \( (x=0) \)?

3. A heavy, non-uniform horizontal plank of length \( L \) has a linear weight density given by \( w(x) = w_0 \left(1 + \frac{x^2}{L^2}\right) \), where \( x = 0 \) is the left end. If the plank is supported symmetrically by two tressels located at its ends, find the upward vertical force exerted by the right support to maintain equilibrium.

4. A circular plate of radius \( R \) has a non-uniform surface mass density that varies with the radial distance from the center, given by \( \sigma(r) = \sigma_0 \left(1 - \frac{r}{R}\right) \). To find where the center of mass lies, you calculate total mass. What is the total mass \( M \) of this plate?

5. A rod of length \( L \) is pivoting freely about its left end \( x = 0 \). A variable upward force per unit length, \( f(x) = \beta x(L - x) \), is applied along the rod. What total concentrated downward force \( F_0 \) must be applied at the far right end \( x = L \) to prevent the rod from rotating?

6. A structural beam extending from \( x=0 \) to \( x=L \) is subject to a wind-induced torque density given by \( \frac{d\tau}{dx} = \gamma \sin\left(\frac{\pi x}{2L}\right) \). What is the magnitude of the total torque acting on the beam about the origin \( x=0 \)?

7. A non-uniform rod of length \( L \) is balanced on a razor edge placed exactly at its midpoint, \( x = \frac{L}{2} \). If the density for the left half \( (0 \le x \le \frac{L}{2}) \) is \( \lambda(x) = \lambda_0 \), what must be the linear density function for the right half \( (\frac{L}{2} \le x \le L) \) to maintain perfect rotational balance if it follows a linear form \( \lambda(x) = C \cdot (L - x) \)?

8. A uniform thin sheet of metal is cut into the shape of a parabola bounded by \( y = kx^2 \), the x-axis, and the line \( x = b \). To determine its rotational balancing characteristics about the y-axis, find the x-coordinate of its center of mass \( (X_{cm}) \).

9. An engineer designs a wedge-shaped support arm whose width increases exponentially: \( w(x) = w_0 e^{x/L} \) from \( x = 0 \) to \( x = L \). Assuming uniform thickness and material density, what is the distance from the narrow end \( (x=0) \) to its center of gravity?

10. A sign of mass \( M \) is suspended horizontally from a wall using a hinge at the left end and a cable at the right end. A variable torque is introduced due to a localized pile of snow accumulating on the sign with a weight density of \( w(x) = \alpha x^3 \) (where \( x=0 \) is the hinge). What is the tension force vector's contribution to torque balance if the cable makes an angle of \( 90^\circ \) to the sign at \( x=L \)?
