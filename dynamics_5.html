Advanced Dynamics Quiz: Calculus-Based Physics

Complete the following 10 problems. Show your work on a separate sheet where integration or differentiation is required.

1. A particle of mass \( m \) moves along the x-axis under the influence of a time-dependent force \( F(t) = F_0 e^{-\alpha t} \), where \( F_0 \) and \( \alpha \) are positive constants. If the particle starts from rest at \( t = 0 \), what is its velocity \( v(t) \) as a function of time?

2. An object of mass \( m \) experiences a position-dependent force given by \( F(x) = -kx + 3bx^2 \). Find the work done by this force as the object moves from \( x = 0 \) to \( x = d \).

3. A mass \( m \) experiences a velocity-dependent drag force \( F_d = -bv^2 \). If the mass has an initial velocity \( v_0 \) at \( t = 0 \) and no other forces act on it, find the velocity \( v(t) \) as a function of time.

4. A conservative force field is described by the potential energy function \( U(x, y) = 3x^2y - 5y^3 \). What is the \( y \)-component of the force, \( F_y \), acting on a particle in this field?

5. A rocket of initial mass \( M_0 \) ejects exhaust gases at a constant relative speed \( u \) and at a constant mass burn rate \( R = -\frac{dM}{dt} \). Ignoring gravity, if the rocket starts from rest, what is its velocity \( v \) when its mass has decreased to \( M \)?

6. The position of a \( 2.0\text{ kg} \) object is given by \( r(t) = (2t^3)\hat{i} + (5t)\hat{j} \) (in SI units). Find the magnitude of the net force acting on the object at \( t = 2.0\text{ s} \).

7. A mass \( m \) moves along a straight line with an acceleration proportional to its displacement from the origin, given by \( a(x) = -\omega^2 x \). If its velocity at \( x = 0 \) is \( v_0 \), use the calculus identity \( a = v\frac{dv}{dx} \) to find its velocity as a function of position \( x \).

8. A time-varying net force \( F(t) = \beta t^2 \) acts on a mass \( m \) initially at rest. What is the total impulse delivered to the mass from \( t = 0 \) to \( t = T \)?

9. A chain of uniform mass density \( \lambda \) and total length \( L \) hangs vertically over the edge of a smooth, frictionless table. If a length \( x \) of the chain is already hanging over the edge, what is the net downward force acting on the hanging segment due to gravity?

10. A particle moves along a path such that its angular momentum about the origin is given by \( L(t) = \gamma t^3 \hat{k} \). What is the net torque \( \tau(t) \) acting on the particle as a function of time?
